Key Takeaways
-------------

1. **Aethir will accelerate enterprise AI adoption by combining global GPU scaling, institutional capital, token utility expansion, and major v2 upgrades into the most complete decentralized cloud roadmap in the industry**

Strategic Compute Reserve: Fueling Institutional Adoption
---------------------------------------------------------

![]()

‍

A major 2025 milestone was the launch of the [Aethir (ATH) Strategic Compute Reserve](https://aethir.com/blog-posts/predictive-oncology-unveils-ath-digital-asset-treasury-to-power-strategic-compute-reserve-and-democratize-ai-infrastructure) (SCR) in partnership with Predictive Oncology (Nasdaq: POAI). As the first Strategic Compute Reserve in the industry, this planned $344M stake in ATH tokens is intended to bridge institutional capital with decentralized compute, turbocharging Cloud Host onboarding and large-scale enterprise deals.​

1. **Impact**: The SCR is designed to support rapid scaling for new compute cohorts, secure compute availability, and enable frictionless booking for enterprise AI, robotics, and simulation workloads.​
2. **Ecosystem flywheel**: Staked ATH tokens in the Strategic Compute Reserve will drive sustainable, high-yield revenue for Cloud Hosts while powering demand from institutional clients.

The SCR will deploy GPU resources to serve AI enterprises and use any realized revenue to purchase additional ATH, further strengthening the Aethir ecosystem.

For investors seeking AI-aligned upside without direct access to top AI companies, the SCR offers exposure to a digital asset convertible into GPU compute through Nasdaq-listed Predictive Oncology (NASDAQ: POAI). This model is positioned to provide a differentiated way to participate in the broader AI infrastructure economy.

As a GPU aggregator, Aethir’s SCR aims to maintain a dynamic supply pipeline, enabling access to compute at scale and with greater efficiency.

Q1 2026: Expanding Ecosystem Flywheel & Compute Capacity
--------------------------------------------------------

![]()

‍

In Q1 2026, Aethir will significantly expand Cloud Host onboarding and GPU capacity to meet the accelerating demand for high-performance AI infrastructure.

### Introducing Aethir v2 Mainnet: IDC v2 Upgrade

**Aethir v2** brings a next-generation **IDC v2 upgrade** to the Proof-of-Compute system, introducing a more robust account and settlement framework, unified staking mechanics, enhanced provider identity controls, and full on-chain transparency for service fees and rewards. These foundational upgrades significantly increase trust, automate more of the compute economy, and allow the network to scale to millions of containers and enterprise workloads with greater reliability and predictability.

This will introduce various key improvements in how Cloud Hosts, Tenants, and token providers interact with the network, including smoother staking/unstaking, clearer account management, and stronger on-chain verification for service fees and rewards.

**The IDC v2 Mainnet upgrade is the foundation of Aethir’s next evolution**.

It introduces:

1. A redesigned Proof-of-Compute system for stronger on-chain verification
2. Horizontally scalable scheduler modules enabling higher throughput
3. Improved Host/Tenant lifecycle management
4. Smoother staking/unstaking and clearer account management
5. More transparent service fee and reward settlement for Cloud Hosts

IDC v2 strengthens the entire operational backbone of Aethir’s decentralized GPU cloud, improving reliability, performance, and the experience of Hosts, Tenants, and token providers.

### Upgrading the EigenLayer ATH Vault

Alongside the Aethir v2 Mainnet launch, the updated **EigenLayer ATH Vault** will unify cross-chain liquidity across multiple ecosystems. By enabling ATH to be mobilized more efficiently across different chains, the Vault enhances overall capital efficiency, allowing resources from multiple ecosystems to support Cloud Host onboarding with lower barriers and reduced cost.

This accelerates the growth of new compute cohorts and strengthens the network’s total supply capacity. While the Proof-of-Compute system continues to operate on its deployment chain, the Vault serves as the cross-chain liquidity layer that fuels faster scaling and more accessible compute availability across the Aethir ecosystem.

This liquidity upgrade helps fuel Cloud Host expansion and supports sustainable staking incentives across the network.

### Aethir’s Blockchain Migration

Our long-awaited **chain migration** is planned to be in 2026, signaling a major technological expansion for Aethir’s DePIN ecosystem. This will enable flexible cross-chain liquidity and compute settlement for ATH, the powering force behind Aethir’s decentralized GPU cloud, while compute settlement for workloads continues to occur on the IDC deployment chain.

Clients will be able to fund compute bookings with ATH sourced from multiple chains, reducing friction and making it easier to onboard more AI innovators in need of reliable, cost-effective GPU infrastructure. The EigenLayer ATH Vault acts as the cross-chain liquidity layer, enabling ATH to move across ecosystems and indirectly benefiting IDC compute staking on whichever chain IDC is deployed.

### Proof-of-Revenue Reports & Quarterly Compute Impact Report

As passionate advocates of business transparency, we will continue publishing **Revenue reports**, along with a **Quarterly Compute Impact Report** showcasing enterprise workload metrics for Q1 2026.

Additionally, throughout 2026, Aethir will participate in major industry events and conferences to showcase enterprise adoption and share insights on decentralized AI infrastructure solutions.

Q2 2026: Maturity—Institutional AI Onboarding & New Product Launches
--------------------------------------------------------------------

![]()

‍

Aethir’s Strategic Compute Reserve will onboard institutional AI clients and venture partners, signaling the next stage of global growth for decentralized AI infrastructure.

This will be a critical step for the mass adoption of Aethir’s decentralized GPU cloud among enterprises that previously used expensive, centralized cloud infrastructure.

### Aethir Staking Pool Migration

In Q2, Aethir will migrate its current staking pools in favor of the EigenLayer ATH Vault, to enable all ATH stakers to directly contribute to the onboarding of new compute cohorts into Aethir’s DePIN stack.

### Aethir Developer SDK & Compute Reputation Layer for Cloud Hosts

On the technical side, we are preparing to launch the **Aethir Developer SDK** for AI integration, further cementing our commitment to supporting AI development with innovative programming tools. This will be accompanied by the launch of the **Compute Reputation Layer** for Cloud Host reliability scoring.

### AI Workload Marketplace v2

Aethir will introduce the **AI Workload Marketplace v2** for GPU scheduling and AI dataset integration, along with a complete **zk-proof prototype** for compute validation under Aethir v3 R&D.

### Aethir Academy & Broader Ecosystem Initiative Growth

As the Aethir ecosystem continues to grow, we will launch the **Aethir Academy**. This ambitious initiative will include a training hub for Cloud Hosts, developers, and partners, further empowering Aethir’s stakeholders and community members. Alongside this, initiatives like the [**AI Unbundled Alliance**](https://aethir.com/blog-posts/aethir-introduces-ai-unbundled-a-web3-ai-industry-alliance) **and** [**Aethir Forge**](https://aethir.com/aethir-forge) will deepen their activities, combining research, thought leadership, and hands-on builder support to drive more high-quality projects onto the network. The community will also have a **public test environment for community-driven performance testing**. Across all of this, we will maintain a deliberate focus on long-term institutional partnerships with high-conviction partners across sectors and geographies.

The **Quarterly Transparency Report (Q2)** will include on-chain and enterprise metrics to highlight the exact growth path of Aethir’s decentralized GPU cloud.
